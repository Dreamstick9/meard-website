Theme Concept: "Warm Charcoal & Muted Gold"

Architectural principles behind bridging the blue identity mark with gold jacquard weave motifs.

■ Warm Charcoal Canvas

Replaces cold pitch-black with `#18181b` and `#27272a` zinc undertones, evoking warm loom wood and woven shadow. Creates tactile depth without artificial SaaS glare.

◆ Muted Gold Weft

Gold `#D4AF37` acts as physical thread—appearing when instructions fire on local hardware. Used for primary CTAs, titles, and golden animal artwork (`/bird.png`).

● Soft Blue Ambient Glow

A radial light field around `/logo.png` softly radiates outward, forming a seamless chromatic transition between the fluid blue monogram and the golden animal motifs.
