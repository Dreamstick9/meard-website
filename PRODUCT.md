## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: funders, institutional partners, and mission-aligned sponsors evaluating whether Meard is a serious non-profit AI research lab worth supporting or partnering with.

Secondary: engineers and builders who will open GitHub, try harness tools, and contribute; researchers assessing collaboration.

## Product Purpose

Meard is a non-profit AI lab. It researches the full stack around language models—not only the model itself, but **harness engineering** (how models are run, routed, evaluated, and integrated)—and intends to **fine-tune and post-train models for specific use cases**. Core belief: **models people use should run on their own hardware**.

Success for this site: a first-time visitor (especially a funder or partner) understands what Meard is, why local-first harness research matters, sees real open work as proof, and can either explore GitHub tools or make contact.

## Positioning

A non-profit lab that treats **harness engineering and local-first deployment** as first-class research—alongside (and ahead of shipping) foundation models—so capability is not locked to multi-tenant cloud APIs. Differentiator neighbors cannot copy: open harness tooling on GitHub today, model weights not claimed yet, explicit post-training/specialization roadmap under a non-profit mission.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate mission fit, open-source activity, and contactability from a single marketing site. Engineers jump to public repositories. No multi-tenant SaaS product is the primary offer.

## Product Principles

1. **Local-first is non-negotiable** — prefer language and demos that put hardware and self-hosting at the center.
2. **Show harness work, don’t claim models** — proof is open tools; model roadmap is intent, not inventory.
3. **Earn funder trust with specificity** — real repos, clear mission, honest gaps.
4. **Open by default** — GitHub is a primary proof surface, not a footer afterthought.
5. **No invented authority** — no fake metrics, partners, or research claims.
